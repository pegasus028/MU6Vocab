# Art Brief: *Back on Her Feet* — 12 storybook pages + 1 cover

Source for T.Chris's `gemini-storybook-art` workflow (Gemini app, built-in browser). This is the only specification for the thirteen pictures. Character blocks must be reproduced word for word in every prompt that uses the character; never shortened or varied between pictures. The thirteen images must look as though one illustrator drew them in one sitting.

**Output targets:** one picture per prompt, square 1:1, finished as 800 × 800 px WebP (q82) at
`art/ch1p1.webp`, `art/ch1p2.webp`, `art/ch2p1.webp`, `art/ch2p2.webp`, `art/ch2p3.webp`, `art/ch3p1.webp`, `art/ch3p2.webp`, `art/ch3p3.webp`, `art/ch4p1.webp`, `art/ch4p2.webp`, `art/ch5p1.webp`, `art/ch5p2.webp` and `art/cover.webp`. When a file exists, set the matching page's `img` in `data/story.js` to that path.

**Reference sheets to make first (in this order, pause for Chris after Praew):** REF-Praew, REF-Mali, REF-Yai, REF-CoachNok, REF-DrPloy, REF-KhunDao, REF-KhunFern, then the minor sheets REF-Bua and REF-Mum. Attach to each picture only the sheets its scene names.

**Hard rules for every picture:** all characters are Thai women or girls; no male characters anywhere; Ratchanok Intanon is named in the story but is never drawn; no blood, no needles or syringes, no close-up of any injury or wound; no brand logos; no school crest; no readable text, letters, numbers or logos (signs, boxes, screens, scoreboards, posters and papers are blank colour blocks or unreadable squiggles); full-bleed, no border or frame. Use a completely new composition for each picture. Each named character appears exactly once. Unless the scene text explicitly mentions a crowd or other students, include NO other people besides the named characters.

---

## 1. STYLE LINE (prepend to every prompt)

Warm, clean, modern picture-book illustration: soft coloured-pencil texture with light watercolour washes, clean ink linework and gentle cel shading; soft even lighting, no harsh shadows; never photo-realistic, never a photograph, never a 3D render, never manga, never a flat vector infographic; no sparkle effects, speed lines or floating emotion symbols. Thai setting details: Bangkok in the rainy season, Benjakitti Forest Park's elevated skywalk and wetlands, a neighbourhood pharmacy in a soi with no brand names, a calm hospital A and E bay. Every face fully visible, never cut off by the frame. Square 1:1.

## 2. CHARACTER BLOCKS (copy exactly)

**Praew (heroine, 13, in every page picture and the cover).** A thirteen-year-old Thai schoolgirl, slim and athletic, who clearly looks thirteen and never older. Long straight black hair in a single plait down her back, tied at the end with a plain red ribbon; a soft side-swept fringe. Oval face, warm brown eyes, light golden-tan skin, a small gap between her two front teeth that shows when she grins. No glasses, no jewellery, no make-up. **Signature:** the plait with the red ribbon; no other girl has a plait. She wears EITHER a plain white short-sleeved school blouse with a navy knee-length pleated skirt, white socks and black shoes (pages 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10) OR a plain white sports shirt with navy shorts and white trainers, no crest and no logo (pages 1, 2, 3, 11, 12, cover); on page 4 only, a plain pale hospital gown. From page 5 (ch2p3) to page 10 (ch4p2) her right arm is in a plain blue triangular cloth sling. On pages 11 and 12 there is no sling.

**Mali (little sister, 8; pages 6, 7, 12, cover).** An eight-year-old Thai girl, small and round-cheeked, with black hair in two short bunches tied with plain yellow bands, big dark eyes and a cheeky expression even when ill. Pages 6 and 7: plain pink pyjamas with no print, flushed pink cheeks. Page 12 and cover: a plain yellow T-shirt and denim shorts. Noticeably smaller than Praew; never a plait.

**Yai (grandmother; pages 9, 12).** A Thai grandmother in her seventies, small and upright, grey hair in a neat bun, kind lined face, reading glasses pushed up on her head. She wears a pale green cotton blouse and a plain dark-blue patterned cotton sarong, and flat sandals. Signature: the bun and the glasses on her head.

**Coach Nok (PE teacher and badminton coach; pages 1, 3, 12).** A fit Thai woman in her thirties, short black hair cut above the shoulders, tanned skin, strong shoulders, a whistle on a plain cord around her neck. She wears a plain navy tracksuit (jacket and trousers, no stripes, no logo) and white trainers. Signature: short hair and the whistle.

**Dr Ploy (A and E doctor; page 4).** A Thai woman doctor in her forties, calm face, black hair in a neat low ponytail, a white coat over a plain blue blouse, a stethoscope around her neck, no name badge. Signature: the white coat and the low ponytail.

**Khun Dao (pharmacist; page 8).** A Thai woman in her thirties, black hair in a tidy bun, round silver-framed glasses, a white pharmacist's coat over a plain lilac blouse. Signature: round glasses and bun (Praew never wears glasses).

**Khun Fern (physiotherapist; page 11).** A Thai woman in her late twenties, athletic, black hair in a high ponytail, a plain teal polo shirt and dark trousers, trainers. Signature: high ponytail and teal shirt.

**Bua (Praew's doubles partner, 13; pages 2, 3, 12, minor).** A Thai schoolgirl the same age as Praew, slightly taller, with a short black bob to the chin and no fringe, in the identical plain white sports shirt and navy shorts. Easy to tell apart: Bua has a bob, Praew has the plait.

**Mum (Praew's mother; pages 4, 5, 6, minor).** A Thai woman in her forties, shoulder-length black hair tucked behind her ears, a plain cream blouse and dark trousers, a small watch, a gentle worried face.

## 3. THE THIRTEEN PICTURES

Each prompt = Style line + the character blocks named + the scene below + the hard rules.

### Cover — `art/cover.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-Mali, REF-Bua.* Scene: Praew in her sports kit stands centre on a badminton court, racket resting on her shoulder, grinning with the gap in her teeth, the red ribbon of her plait catching the light; Bua behind her left shoulder laughing; Mali at the front hugging Praew's leg and waving a shuttlecock. Behind them, soft and out of focus, the green of Benjakitti Park and a grey-blue rainy-season sky. Leave calm empty space in the upper third for a title to be added later in the app (the image itself carries no text). Palette: warm whites, navy, red ribbon, soft green.

### Page 1 (ch1p1) — Warm-up on the skywalk — `art/ch1p1.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-CoachNok, REF-Bua.* Dawn on the elevated skywalk in Benjakitti Forest Park, Bangkok, under a grey rainy-season sky with wet green wetlands and the city skyline behind. Coach Nok stands at the front demonstrating a stretch with her hands on her hips. Four schoolgirls in plain white sports shirts and navy shorts stretch in a line along the walkway (a crowd of four is allowed here: Praew, Bua and two unnamed teammates who have plain ponytails). Praew is at the end of the line, half-turned to chat to Bua instead of stretching her shoulders. Tint: bright.

### Page 2 (ch1p2) — The dive — `art/ch1p2.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-Bua.* Indoor badminton court in a Bangkok school gym, bright overhead lights, green court surface. Praew is frozen mid-dive, stretched horizontally towards a shuttlecock just over the net, racket extended, one shoulder about to touch the floor. Behind her Bua has a hand over her mouth. On the far side of the net, two opposing girls in plain pale-blue kit watch (allowed: the scene names them). A simple scoreboard on the wall shows blank squares. Dynamic but not violent; no injury shown; no blood. Tint: cool.

### Page 3 (ch2p1) — Calling 1669 — `art/ch2p1.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-CoachNok, REF-Bua.* Inside the same gym by the open double doors. Praew, in her sports kit, sits on the floor looking pale and tearful, cradling her right arm against her body. Coach Nok kneels in front of her, gently lifting Praew's chin and looking into her eyes with a small pen torch. Bua stands behind holding a phone to her ear. Through the doors a white ambulance with a plain unmarked side waits in the rain. No wound, no blood, no close-up of the shoulder. Tint: cool.

### Page 4 (ch2p2) — The X-ray — `art/ch2p2.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-DrPloy, REF-Mum.* A calm hospital A and E bay in Bangkok, pale green curtains, soft light. Dr Ploy holds a grey shoulder X-ray film up against a glowing lightbox and points at it with a reassuring smile; the X-ray is a simple pale schematic of a shoulder, nothing graphic. Praew sits up on the bed in a plain pale hospital gown, looking relieved and slightly sheepish. Mum sits beside the bed holding Praew's left hand. No needles, no drips, no blood. Tint: cool.

### Page 5 (ch2p3) — Home in a sling — `art/ch2p3.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-Mum.* Night-time back seat of a pink Bangkok taxi in the rain, city lights blurred through the wet window; the taxi has no markings. Praew, now in her white blouse and navy skirt, sits with her right arm in a plain blue triangular cloth sling and a small round star sticker on her collar, looking out of the window with a thoughtful, slightly regretful face. Mum sits beside her holding a plastic bag of folded blank leaflets and patting Praew's knee. Cosy warm interior light. Tint: warm.

### Page 6 (ch3p1) — Mali's bad day — `art/ch3p1.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-Mali, REF-Mum.* A cosy Bangkok family living room, evening, rain on the window. Mali, in plain pink pyjamas, lies limply along the sofa under a light blanket with a flushed pink face and half-closed eyes. Mum sits on the edge of the sofa frowning gently at a plain digital thermometer whose display is a blank glow. Praew, in blouse and skirt with her right arm in the blue sling, watches worriedly from an armchair. A box of tissues on the table. Tint: warm.

### Page 7 (ch3p2) — Achoo! — `art/ch3p2.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-Mali.* Praew's bedroom, daytime, posters as plain coloured rectangles. Mali, in pink pyjamas, sits cross-legged on the bed mid-sneeze, eyes squeezed shut, a tissue in her hand, cheeks flushed; sheets of homework paper with only faint lines fly off the bed. Praew, blouse and skirt, right arm in the blue sling, stands by the desk with a horrified comic expression, holding out a plain white face mask towards her sister with her left hand. A glass of water and a box of tissues on the bedside table. Tint: bright.

### Page 8 (ch3p3) — At the chemist's — `art/ch3p3.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-KhunDao.* Inside a small bright neighbourhood pharmacy in a Bangkok soi: white shelves of plain unlabelled boxes and bottles in soft colours, a glass door with one small round green sticker (no writing). Khun Dao leans over the counter explaining kindly, holding a small plain white box in one hand and a face mask in the other. Praew, blouse and skirt, right arm in the blue sling, listens and nods, a small shopping basket on the counter. All packaging is plain colour; no brand names. Tint: cool.

### Page 9 (ch4p1) — Yai's cabinet — `art/ch4p1.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-Yai.* A warm Thai family kitchen with an old wooden cabinet open to show rows of jars, small bottles, dried herbs and tins, all unlabelled. Yai stands at the cabinet holding up a tiny plain inhaler tube in one hand and a steaming mug of ginger tea in the other, with a proud, stubborn smile. Praew, blouse and skirt, right arm in the blue sling, sits at the table reading the back of a small plain box with a thoughtful frown; a plain sports-drink bottle and a few small plain sachets sit in front of her. Fresh ginger and limes on the counter. Tint: warm.

### Page 10 (ch4p2) — From scratch — `art/ch4p2.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew.* Praew's bedroom at night, desk lamp glowing. Praew, blouse and skirt, right arm in the blue sling, sits at her desk typing one-handed on a laptop with a blank glowing screen. Propped beside her is a hand-drawn science poster showing, in simple pictures only, a flooded town, a pot of water boiling over a fire, a willow tree with drooping branches and a single plain white round tablet. Through the open door, Mali's small bed is empty and neatly made; a small packed overnight bag with a plain yellow toy sits by the door. Quiet warm night palette. Tint: cool.

### Page 11 (ch5p1) — Physio — `art/ch5p1.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-KhunFern.* A bright clean physiotherapy room with a window onto green trees, a padded bench and a wall poster of simple stick-figure exercise pictures (no words). Khun Fern stands beside Praew gently guiding her elbow. Praew, in her sports kit with no sling, pulls a plain yellow rubber resistance band outwards with her right arm, face scrunched with effort and pride. A folded blue sling lies discarded on the bench. Tint: bright.

### Page 12 (ch5p2) — The final — `art/ch5p2.webp`
*Refs: REF-Praew, REF-Bua, REF-CoachNok, REF-Mali, REF-Yai.* A school badminton final in a Bangkok gym, warm afternoon light through high windows; a small crowd of schoolgirls in plain white blouses and navy skirts in the stands (allowed: the scene names a crowd; none of them has a plait). On court, Praew (sports kit, no sling, both arms raised with her racket) and Bua hug with arms around each other's shoulders, both laughing. Coach Nok claps at the side of the court. In the front row Mali, in a plain yellow T-shirt, jumps up waving a plain red banner with no writing, and Yai beams beside her. The scoreboard is blank. Tint: warm.

## 4. QA checklist per picture
1. Praew: plait + red ribbon, gap teeth, correct outfit for the page, sling present on pages 5–10 only and on the RIGHT arm.
2. No male figure anywhere; no second girl with a plait.
3. No readable text, numbers, logos, crest or brand marks; scoreboard, boxes, screens, posters and papers blank.
4. No blood, wound, needle, syringe or drip; no close-up of the shoulder injury.
5. Named characters appear exactly once; no unrequested crowds.
6. Square, full-bleed; finish at 800 × 800 WebP q82 under the file name above.
